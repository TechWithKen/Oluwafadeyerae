Chapter 1

Chapter 01

How It All Began

On a platform called Barracks

“In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.”

John 1:1

And somewhere in the middle of all that, there was you. ❤️

Funny how life works. Sometimes the most beautiful beginnings come from moments that seem ordinary at first.

Our story began on a platform called Barracks, where we were both taking part in a 30-day journey of fasting and prayer. At the end of the programme, participants began introducing themselves, sharing pictures, where they were based, and little details about who they were.

You did your introduction that morning, but I missed it. I had muted everyone’s statuses, so your introduction passed me by completely.

Then came the evening.

I was talking with my close friend, Kike, when she mentioned she had posted something about me. Naturally, I went to check. And while I was going through the statuses, I stumbled upon your introduction from that morning.

And then… who was this girl? 😂❤️

Let me be honest. Your smile caught my attention first, then your teeth. Yes, your teeth deserve their own special mention, because they clearly made quite an impression on me. Then came your face card, which wasn’t helping matters either. 😂

Before I knew it, curiosity had taken over. Who was this beautiful girl? What was she like? What would talking to her feel like?

I wanted your number, but then I thought: why collect it from someone else when I could get it directly from the group chat? So I went looking for your introduction and eventually found my way into your messages.

And just like that, we started talking.

I had no idea what would come of that first message. I certainly didn’t imagine that the girl whose introduction I almost missed would become someone I’d write a whole love letter to.

But here we are. And I’m glad I found that status, discovered your smile, and decided to say hello.

Who would have thought that something so simple could become the beginning of a story I’d be this happy to tell?

Chapter 02

The Unexpected Journey

The chat I deleted, and came back to

When we started talking, you seemed cool and calm. At least you were replying to my messages, so I counted that as a good start. 😂

Gradually, we became familiar with each other, but our beginning wasn’t entirely smooth.

There was that time you were busy trying to sort some things out. I kept asking whether you were free, and you told me not to ask you that question again.

And me? I felt like you had shouted at me. 😂

Honestly, I didn’t want to talk anymore. I think that’s what made me delete our chat, even though I had told myself I wouldn’t.

The funniest part is that I had already been telling Kike about you. Imagine talking about a girl to your friend, then deleting your conversation with that same girl because you felt some type of way. 😂

But I think I was trying not to get attached. I had things I wanted to focus on, goals I wanted to pursue, and a life I was still figuring out. Getting emotionally invested in someone wasn’t exactly part of the plan.

So I deleted the chat, thinking that would be the end of it.

Except it wasn’t.

I came back, even after telling myself I wouldn’t text you again. Somehow, I found my way back into your messages, and we kept talking.

Looking back, I find that part of our story especially funny. I was trying so hard not to get attached, yet I kept returning to the very conversation I had decided to leave behind.

Imagine if I had never texted again. One small misunderstanding, and that could have been the end of a connection that has brought me so much happiness.

I’m not saying we’ll never misunderstand each other again. We’re different people, and there will always be things to learn about one another. But I’m glad I gave myself another chance to know you, instead of letting one moment define the whole experience.

And somewhere between those conversations, the awkward moments, and our growing familiarity, this connection began to mean something to me.

What started as curiosity about a beautiful girl became a genuine interest in the person behind the pictures.

And now, almost a month later, here I am, wondering how the girl I almost didn’t get to know became someone I want to be intentional about.

Funny how things turn out, isn’t it?

Chapter 03

The Things I Love About You

Let me count the ways

Now, let’s talk about you.

As Elizabeth Barrett Browning asks in her famous sonnet:

“How do I love thee? Let me count the ways.”

Sonnet 43

And honestly, beautiful girl, where do I even begin? ❤️

Of course, your smile, your teeth, and your face card deserve their flowers. 😂 But beyond all that, there’s so much about you that I genuinely appreciate.

I love your humour and the little things that make our conversations enjoyable. I love your curiosity, and your willingness to explore ideas and discover new things. I think it would be beautiful to have someone I can laugh with over the most unserious things one moment, and have meaningful conversations with the next.

I love that you enjoy movies and series. I look forward to sharing those with you, even if we occasionally have to negotiate what we’re watching. 😂

I appreciate your taste in music, too, even though I’m still not convinced you have any. Please, let’s leave that discussion for another day. 😂

More importantly, I admire your relationship with God. I love that you love Him and want to grow in Him.

That means a lot to me, because I want a relationship where we can encourage each other spiritually, learn together, and stay intentional about the values that guide our lives.

I know neither of us has everything figured out, but I appreciate your desire to grow spiritually, and I would love for us to nurture that together.

I also admire your humility, kindness, thoughtfulness, and generosity. I appreciate your elegance, the way you carry yourself, and the calm I associate with your presence. There’s something beautiful about getting to know someone whose personality gives you reasons to appreciate them beyond physical attraction.

And then there’s your business-minded side. I love that you can be particular and strategic about your business. It’s lovely to see that you have your own interests, ambitions, and things you care about.

Physically, I love your eyes, your smile, your teeth (yes, those teeth again), and your beauty. And let’s not forget your height. 😂❤️

I also have to commend your taste in men. Honestly, excellent taste. I mean, look at me. What more evidence do we need? 😂😂

But jokes aside, what I appreciate most is that you are so much more than a pretty face or a list of admirable qualities.

You’re a person with your own thoughts, dreams, interests, and experiences, and I’m excited to discover more of what makes you who you are.

I know we still have plenty to learn about each other, but I already appreciate the person I’m getting to know.

Thank you for the love and care you’ve shown me, for the conversations we’ve shared, and for making this beginning such a beautiful experience.

You came into my life when I wasn’t looking for a relationship, yet knowing you has made me reconsider what I thought I wanted.

And that’s something I don’t take lightly.

Chapter 04

Will You Take This Ride With Me?

The bold step

Before meeting you, I had decided that love wasn’t something I wanted to pursue at this point in my life. I had goals to accomplish, dreams to chase, and things I was still trying to figure out.

I thought I would leave relationships for another time.

Then I met you.

Meeting you hasn’t suddenly answered every question I have about life, but it has made me reconsider the possibility of sharing the journey with someone.

I’ve realised that I don’t need every part of my life perfectly arranged before I can choose to build something meaningful with another person.

And you are someone I would love to build with.

I want us to grow together, make an impact, and encourage each other in our individual pursuits. I want us to explore new places, visit restaurants and cinemas, go on adventures, watch movies, and create memories that will make us laugh when we look back on them.

I want us to get curious together, learn from each other, and have conversations that challenge our thinking. I want us to make wise financial decisions, pursue opportunities, and work towards a future we can both be proud of.

I also want us to keep growing spiritually, encouraging each other in faith while building a relationship grounded in honesty, respect, patience, and understanding.

I don’t expect us to be perfect. I know there will be things to learn, differences to navigate, and moments that will ask us to be more patient with each other.

But I want us to meet those moments with a willingness to communicate, understand, and grow.

I want us to be lovers and friends: two people who can laugh together, talk openly, support each other’s ambitions, and enjoy the beautiful things life has to offer without losing sight of what truly matters.

And the truth is, I want to experience all of that with you.

I know we’ve only been getting to know each other for a short time. There’s still so much about you I haven’t discovered, and I won’t pretend otherwise. But I believe this connection is worth exploring intentionally, and I’m willing to take that bold step.

So, beautiful girl, this is me telling you that I love you, I appreciate you, and I would love to be with you.

I’m not promising you a perfect story. I’m asking for the chance to build a meaningful one together.

Perhaps one day we’ll look back at how it all started and laugh about it: Barracks, the 30 days of fasting and prayer, the introduction I almost missed, the smile that caught my attention, the messages, the misunderstanding, and the boy who deleted a chat only to come back again.

And maybe we’ll smile at the fact that what began with a simple introduction became something we both chose to nurture.

I’m glad I sent that first message. And now, I want to find out what the next chapter could look like with you.

So, here’s my question.

Would you like to take this ride with me?

Would you like us to grow together, learn together, laugh together, pray together, explore the world together, and build something beautiful?

Would you like to give us a chance?

❤️🥹

With love, sincerity, and excitement for what we could become,

Yours, hopefully, in the chapters to come. ❤️
